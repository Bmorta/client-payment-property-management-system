# Partial contract cancellation, payment credits and refunds

## Location and approval workflow

Open **Clients → Client Account → Properties → Cancellations, Payment Credits & Refunds**. Administrators can prepare cases, approve value, finalize cancellation and record actual refunds. Other authenticated roles can view case history and report previews without financial action controls.

1. Select only the contracts to cancel (up to 20). Review original principal/DP allocations, penalties, balances and receipts. Other contracts remain independent.
2. Select 30% of eligible actual principal/DP, up to 100%, or a custom amount. Custom/reduced full-value choices require justification. These are payment-credit/refund policies, not discounts to any selling price. Paid penalties and other charges are not principal. Unknown or unreconciled penalty policies must be resolved first; the system does not assume zero.
3. Choose unpaid-penalty deduction, documented waiver, or prior separate settlement. Deduction is taken once from the proposed value. Negative entitlement is rejected. Record the legal/contractual basis, reviewed minimum value, review reference and whether installments are permitted. Confirm prior credits/refunds, including external releases, have been reconciled.
4. Allocate value to retained contracts owned by the same client, refund installments, or both. Source-level allocations share one capped entitlement. A reviewed statutory cash minimum is reserved in the refund payable before noncash credits. Remaining approved value requires a documented disposition. Percentage retention, penalty deduction and unused approved value are shown separately.
5. Review receiving schedules: keep the current monthly amount and shorten the term; keep the remaining unpaid periods and recompute monthly; or authorize a new term/date. Down-payment credit is handled first. Schedules reconcile exactly in integer centavos, including the last installment. Receiving penalties must already be separately settled/waived; they are not silently converted to principal or erased.
6. **Approve & Reserve** creates an approved pending case and refund payable. Selected sources are held against duplicate disposition; their cash receipts/entitlement are frozen for review. They are not yet finally cancelled. Other retained accounts remain payable, and credits are not posted yet.
7. Record actual refund releases separately, with date, amount, method/funding and recipient. Reference/remarks are optional. Each release has a unique RFD ID and append-only actor/source allocations. There is no automatic payout. Single-release agreements cannot be bypassed by posting partial releases; installment splits require explicit reviewed permission.
8. Review final cancellation requirements and a fresh receiving schedule. Source payments cannot change while reserved. Receiving accounts may have received real payments meanwhile, so their latest balance/version is rechecked. Receiving allocations may be redistributed only within the approved total credit. Confirm documented requirements; applicable statutory cases cannot finalize until their approved cash refund is fully released. Noncash settlement evidence is separately required where applicable.
9. **Finalize Cancellation & Apply Credits** atomically closes only selected source contracts, applies linked noncash amendments to existing retained contracts, and updates inventory. Sources remain Unavailable unless clearance was requested and is explicitly reconfirmed at finalization. Finalization uses the same transaction for all accounts and lots; stale/concurrent changes roll it back.
10. Continue recording any permitted voluntary-settlement refund installments. A case becomes SETTLED only after final cancellation and release of its entire approved refund payable. Refund completion alone does not automatically cancel a contract.

## Financial safeguards and records

- CAN case IDs and RFD release IDs are system-generated. Submission IDs, a unique source-contract claim, account versions and preview tokens prevent reuse/double submission. Combined credits/refunds never exceed approved value. Actual releases never exceed the remaining refund payable.
- Original receipts, corrections, contracts and payouts remain on their original accounts. No collection is copied, deleted or manufactured. Credits are dated principal adjustments; receiving contract selling prices remain unchanged.
- Receiving amendments retain the ledger before their effective date and replay only subsequent actual receipts against the revised collectible schedule. Pre-amendment installment identities and explicitly paid future periods remain traceable. Multiple amendments and historical as-of balances are supported. Underlying receipts cannot be silently corrected after their value has been relied on; a reviewed reconciliation is required.
- Commission eligibility remains tied to actual allocated cash principal against the original agreement. Original period identities and payouts remain intact; future cash fills the remaining original commission basis. Credit never earns commission. Pending vouchers, recovery balances and historical payout-verification issues must be resolved before approval/amendment. The case captures reconciliation snapshots; later legitimate original-agent payouts remain in the live commission ledger.
- Penalty deductions/waivers are recorded as cancellation settlements, not fictitious cash penalty receipts. No refund is silently issued for earlier penalty payments.
- Refund approval creates a payable only. Actual CASH-funded releases reduce complete-office cash once; GCash/bank/noncash releases do not. Refunds are not automatically created as expenses and are reported separately from collection-minus-expense totals. Possible duplicate manually encoded cash expenses are flagged for reconciliation.
- Cash-funded refunds require a verified opening baseline, classified records and sufficient cash across the historical daily timeline. Concurrent office cash movements serialize through the existing guard. Invalid/insufficient-cash releases roll back both the payout and payable update. There is no automatic cash refund, agent-payment reversal, forfeiture, lot release or contract reinstatement.
- Case history, account audit entries and the activity journal retain actors, timestamps, reasons and before/after financial snapshots. All notifications/confirmations/errors use centered body-level dialogs. Closing an unsaved preparation requires confirmation.

## Reports

Open a case to preview its shared report, then Print Preview or Export Excel. Both render the same sections: source entitlements/DP/penalties/legal review; allocations and disposition; original/revised schedules; refund plan; actual releases; original receipts/revisions; source/receiving commissions; and audit history. Stale export snapshots require refresh.

Collection & Cash Summary also lists actual refunds by source contract, including method and physical/noncash funding. Print, Excel and existing PDF exports contain the same refund detail and cash totals. Refunds are already included once in other physical cash outflows; do not deduct them again as an expense. Payment schedules identify noncash credit amendments and closed/pending cancellation cases.

## Operational limits and review requirements

MongoDB transaction support is mandatory for approval, finalization and refund releases. Unsupported standalone installations return an error without changing financial records. New approvals use today’s Asia/Manila date; original payment dates are retained. Snapshot size limits require splitting exceptionally large batches.

The existing active-contract index upgrade must be completed before a cancelled lot can be released for resale. The script now recognizes cancelled historical contracts. No live index upgrade was performed during development. See `lot-transfers.md` for the preview/apply procedure with a verified backup.

Inherited-credit source contracts are flagged for lineage reconciliation instead of being automatically turned into a second refund entitlement. Unknown penalty terms, migration drafts, pending payment corrections, inconsistent ownership and approved forfeiture states also require review. Receiving inherited credits is supported. Ordinary agreement edits/commission agreement changes cannot overwrite an approved credit basis. Case withdrawal, credit reversal and correction/recovery of an erroneous actual refund require a separately reviewed financial adjustment workflow; there is no destructive Delete or silent undo.

This module records administrator-reviewed legal inputs; it is not an automatic legal-eligibility engine. Company policy must not override buyer protections. RA 6552 provides qualifying buyers cash-surrender and cancellation safeguards; applicability, notices and settlement evidence must be reviewed outside the calculation engine and referenced here: https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/2/1688 . An installment refund agreement alone is not treated as permission to finalize statutory cancellation.
